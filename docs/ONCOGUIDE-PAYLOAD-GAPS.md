# OncoGuide to OnCo payload coverage

Updated 2026-11-08. The initial local audit identified a five-class payload comparison registry and a single drug payload string. The content expansion is now implemented as 31 sourced glossary concepts, connected to existing technologies and drug records.

The full [research reader](../research/payloads-2026-11-08/README.md) includes Elicit queries and JSON responses, resolved primary sources, identifier dispositions and remaining gaps. It expands beyond OncoGuide to Gq/11 and PI3K/PIKK inhibitors, PDD and IGN mono-alkylators, KIF11 degraders, protein alkylators, proteasome inhibition and adjacent delivery formats. The completion search also adds MCL-1 inhibition and a directly conjugated sulfasalazine ferroptosis payload.

Four parent records were revised: payload, dual-payload conjugates, degrader conjugates and immune-stimulating conjugates. The changes remove guarantees of resistance prevention and assumptions of lower degrader potency or toxicity.

The comparison registry and product schema remain unchanged: unknown permeability, loading and toxicity values were not invented to fill required fields. Multiple payload identities, property uncertainty and assertion-level provenance remain future schema work.

The reusable audit method is to map each teaching distinction to an existing entity, find the missing relationship or concept, search beyond the lesson, and verify the primary evidence before adding facts. Apply it next to antigen expression versus internalization, assay validity versus prediction, model activity versus human benefit, and trial eligibility versus access.
