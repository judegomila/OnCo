# UCSF breast-study coverage, October 10, 2026

Source: [UCSF breast cancer studies, female and age 18 or over](https://clinicaltrials.ucsf.edu/breast-cancer#filter=sex-female-AND-age-18-and-over).

The captured page contains 121 study cards. Applying both published card classes (`trials-list--trial-open-to-sex-female` and `trials-list--trial-open-to-age-18-and-over`) includes 120 studies. The male-only NCT01101425 is excluded. Of the included studies, UCSF marks 47 open to eligible people and 73 locally closed. These are local availability labels, not the overall recruitment status in ClinicalTrials.gov. All 120 exact NCT identifiers resolved in the registry API.

At base commit `d69365083ab824134621b7aa9b5903729a8d1db2`, the assembled OnCo graph contains 62 of those NCT identifiers. A graph-wide search of names, aliases and links found no additional matches for the other 58. This patch adds those 58 (22 locally open and 36 locally closed), preserving the canonical identifiers of all 62 existing records. All 120 are linked from the existing UCSF institution record. The existing I-SPY 2 and 2.2 programme pages share NCT01042379; the inventory uses the existing 2.2 page and preserves both programme records.

The [coverage matrix](ucsf-breast-2026-10-10.csv) lists every NCT, its OnCo identifier, whether it existed at base, local availability, overall registry status, registry study type and phase, and direct source URLs. It is an inventory snapshot, not a personalized eligibility assessment. Existing trial facts are not refreshed wholesale by this patch.

The new records distinguish treatment, prevention, screening, supportive care, registries and expanded access in their descriptions. Observational records retain that design; interventional records with phase `NA` and expanded-access records use `not-applicable`, displayed as “Phase not applicable”. This avoids an invented phase or an incorrect observational classification. The two expanded-access records, NCT04395508 and NCT03906331, have registry status `APPROVED_FOR_MARKETING`; this describes the access record and does not assert that the former program remains available. NCT06671912 is suspended and has no invented corpus status; its participation fields and description retain the exact registry status. NCT01570998's narrative calls it phase IV while its structured phase is `NA`; the record names that discrepancy.

Each new record includes a dated participation capture with the SHA-256 of its complete individually fetched registry response. [Source receipts](ucsf-breast-2026-10-10-sources.json) include URLs, fetch times and hashes for the UCSF page, the two complete registry batches and the 58 individual registry responses. Exact source bytes are retained locally outside tracked files; no full eligibility text, site contacts or clinical outcomes have been inferred. Trial descriptions summarize the study questions, not evidence that the treatments work. Estimated enrollment remains in the dated participation fields and qualified summary; only actual counts use the unqualified `enrolled` field, because the current overview renders that field as “enrolled”.

Drug relationships are limited to existing, unambiguously identified OnCo records. Named experimental interventions can still lack their own drug entity; this patch completes study coverage rather than claiming comprehensive drug coverage or complete trial results.

Proposed through [issue #247](https://github.com/judegomila/OnCo/issues/247), pending maintainer triage and clinical/scientific and patient-advocate review. No expert sign-off is claimed. Public sources only; no patient information.
