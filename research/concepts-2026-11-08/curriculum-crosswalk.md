The web and Elicit lanes also led to concepts beyond these lesson matches: biomarker qualification, test agreement versus accuracy, confounding by indication, immortal time bias, target-trial emulation and competing events. The baseline already names several of these in prose; the additions give the distinction its own cited explanation and related links. A missing standalone record does not mean the corpus never mentioned a term.
