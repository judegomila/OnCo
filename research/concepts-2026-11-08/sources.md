# Sources and their scope

Checked 8 November 2026. Publication identities were resolved through Europe PMC and the abstracts supporting new claims were read. This ledger distinguishes experimental studies, clinical studies, original methods, consensus frameworks and a methodological review. None is promoted to a different evidence type. No trial outcome figure or clinical cutoff was copied. [Raw resolution responses](primary/capture-manifest.json) retain exact titles, identifiers and abstracts; [structured ledger](sources.json) maps each source to the records that use it. [Full-text verification points](fulltext-verification.json) supplement selected abstracts.
