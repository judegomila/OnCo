# Primary source reader

Checked 8 November 2026. These are the evidence scopes used for this patch. Exact resolved titles, DOI/PMID pairs, publication types, captures and affected record IDs are in [the source-to-record ledger](source-to-records.json). Bibliographic records and abstracts were read for the cited papers; specific full-text checks are listed in [fulltext-verification.json](fulltext-verification.json). No full-paper review is implied for every citation.
